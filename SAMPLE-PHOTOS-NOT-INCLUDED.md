# Sample photos intentionally not included

In the working app, the Showcase personas' sample photos are embedded as base64 text files (`alex-sample-*.b64.txt`, `koa-sample-*.b64.txt`, `leilani-sample-*.b64.txt`) and imported by `actions.ts`. Those photo payloads are **omitted from this public repo on purpose** — this repo never contains photos of people. Showcase Alex, Showcase Koa, and Showcase Leilani are illustrative personas, not real people.
